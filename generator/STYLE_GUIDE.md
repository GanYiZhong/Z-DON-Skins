# YataiDON SimpleStyle: style guide

Generated assets: `mockups/swatch.png`, `mockups/mascot_concepts.png` (`build.py --mockups`).
The token tables at the end are generated by `ss/tokens.py` on every build. Do not edit them by hand.

## Direction (user decisions 2026-10-28)
- **Dark, flat "slate".** No gradients on shapes, no bevels, no drop shadows. Each colour has one job. The lane and the notes carry the contrast; everything else stays in the background.
- **Mascot: "Tick"**, an original metronome robot drawn by code (`ss/mascot.py`, concept A).
  - It has an off-white soft-trapezoid body, a dark screen face with sky-blue eyes, and a pendulum with an amber weight.
  - It replaces the character everywhere Green shows one. The 3D Don stays off (`disable_chara_3d`).
  - Concepts B ("Puff", cloud) and C ("Fuu", eighth note) remain buildable with `--mascot B|C`.
- **Fixed text is Japanese** (良 / 可 / 不可 …). Where Green ships `_en` texture variants, we ship them too. The base key is Japanese; the engine falls back ja → en for other languages.
- **No extra colour-blind marks** on notes.
- **Every screen keeps Green's layout and Lua.** Only the graphics are restyled. Lua is patched only where Green-specific art has to go.

## Spacing, radius, stroke (one scale)
- **Spacing:** 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 28 / 40 px. Card padding is 28, the screen gutter is 40, and the gap between cards is 24.
- **Radius:**
  - 4: chips and small bars
  - 8: buttons and pills (pills are fully rounded)
  - 16: cards and panels
  - 20: large boxes
  - circles for notes, badges and the mascot shadow
- **Stroke:**
  - 1 px hairline: `border.subtle`
  - 2 px separation outline: `surface.base`, used around notes and on overlapping shapes
  - 3 px focus ring: `accent.gold`
  - The note rim is 15 % of the radius, in `on-surface.primary`.

## Typography
- **Fonts** (all SIL OFL 1.1; see `Fonts/OFL.txt`):
  - Noto Sans JP Bold → `font.ttf`
  - Noto Sans TC Bold → `font_tw.ttf`
  - Noto Sans SC Regular → `font_cn.ttf` (the only SC cut available locally, converted from CFF to TrueType)
  - Noto Sans KR Bold → `font_kr.ttf` (converted from CFF to TrueType)
- **How the engine picks a font:** `font_<lang>`, then `font_<family>` (zh→cn, zh_tw→tw, ko→kr, ja→jp), then `font.ttf`, looked up in the child skin and then the parent.
- **Texture text** is drawn with Noto Sans JP at weights 700–900.
- **Sizes:**
  - Labels: 18 px, `on-surface.secondary`
  - Values: 28 / 40 px
  - Titles: 34–40 px
- **Numbers are always tabular.** Every digit is drawn in a fixed cell (`ss/ui.py` digits_s/m/l: 0-9 + - . % : /), so counters never jitter. The Lua helper `UI.num()` lays them out.

## Motion
- Allow at most 1–2 animated elements per view. Motion must mean something, and exits are faster than entries.
- **Gameplay:**
  - The mascot is the only ambient motion. It squashes on each beat and its pendulum swings once per beat.
  - It reacts with a 220 ms good face and a 900 ms combo-100 face.
  - Persistent moods: sad (miss streak), gogo, clear/fail at the end.
  - The engine's own judge pops and gauge animations are kept.
- **No decorative loops:** no dancers, no confetti beyond the parent FC script, no breathing backgrounds. The phase-1 rings are removed.

## Gameplay lower half (1P, GAME screen only)
- **Info panel**, two `surface.panel` cards at y 422–658:
  - **Left card:** the song title and subtitle, then a row of course + ★level (when known from our song select), live BPM, and soul-gauge %.
  - **Right card:** 良 / 可 / 不可 / 連打 counts, max combo, accuracy (good=1, ok=0.5) with a thin bar, and the average timing offset. The offset appears only when the engine's `show_timing_offset` config is on.
- **Hidden on:** 2P (the 2P lane is there), dan, practice and AI screens. They draw their own panels.
- **Data sources:** only the background callbacks and `tex.*`. See the report for proposed engine bindings: per-hit offset, song progress, course/level without song select.

## Shapes
- **Notes:** a flat disk, white rim, 2 px dark outline. Small notes have r 36 and big notes r 54. Centres and sizes match the parent, so engine offsets are unchanged.
- **Drumroll:** an amber band with white edges and a half-disk tail.
- **Balloon:** an orange head, a white string and a small balloon.
- **Kusudama:** an amber ball with a seam.
- **Difficulty emblem:** a coloured circle with a geometric glyph. Easy–Oni show 1–4 dots. Ura is a ring, Tower is stacked bars, and Dan is a square.
- **Gauge:** a dark groove tinted with the player colour, a gold clear zone and a solid fill. Rainbow is a flat hue sweep.
