# YataiDONSimpleStyle

A light, 8-bit skin for YataiDON. Version 2 is built on the layouts and screen flow of
**YataiDONNijiiro** (its parent skin) and redraws every screen in one game-like look: a cream screen
under a pale sky band, a festival stage with a taiko and lantern strings, white windows with a thick
ink border, notched corners and a hard drop shadow, segmented pixel meters and a pixel font. Notes and
the lane stay flat and smooth; everything else sits on one pixel grid.

Windows open and close in a few whole frames, and blinking items (the title's start prompt, the
cursors) switch fully on and off instead of fading. The control hint is one ink bar at the bottom right
of the screen.

## Resolution

- **4K (main build).** On Z-DON the skin uses `screen.texture_density = 2`: layouts stay in the
  parent's 1920x1080 coordinates, while every texture is authored at twice the size and the game
  renders at 3840x2160. Pixel art stays sharp at 4K.
- **1080p build.** Engines without the texture-density feature (for example upstream YataiDON) need the
  plain build, made with `python build.py --density 1`. It contains the same art at 1920x1080.

## Characters

Two original pixel characters appear throughout the skin:

- **Momo**, a pink rabbit with a mint belly: the main character and player 1.
- **Hoshi**, a husky pup in a grey hoodie: the buddy and player 2.

They stand on the ground or on a window edge, face what they act on, drum, cheer and blink. They never
stretch or rotate: they move by whole sprite pixels or change frames.

## Fonts

- **Fusion Pixel Font 12px** (https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font), licensed under the
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence texts are in `Fonts/OFL.txt` and `Fonts/LICENSES/`.
  All text, baked or drawn by the game, uses this one family at 18 / 24 / 36 / 48 px.

## Contents

- `Graphics/`: every texture this skin shows, drawn from scratch by a generator script. The title and
  settings screens also ship their own `animation.json`: the start prompt blinks hard, there is no white
  screen fade after the start, and the settings arrows hold still at full opacity.
- `Scripts/`: Lua scripts. Some are adapted from the parent skins (YataiDONNijiiro / PyTaikoGreen) with
  their art and timelines replaced; credit for the adapted parts goes to their authors. The rest are
  original.
- `Videos/`: empty folders, so the title screen plays the skin's own animated scene instead of videos.
