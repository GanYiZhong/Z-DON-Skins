# YataiDONSimpleStyleDark

The night version of **YataiDONSimpleStyle**: the same layouts, characters and 8-bit pixel style, in a
dark look. Dark screens and windows instead of the cream screen and white windows, light text and
borders, and a starry night sky instead of the pale sky band. Notes and the lane look the same as in
SimpleStyle.

This skin needs **YataiDONSimpleStyle** installed: it is the parent skin (`"parent":
"YataiDONSimpleStyle"` in `Graphics/skin_config.json`), and its sounds come from it.
SimpleStyle in turn builds on **YataiDONNijiiro**.

## Resolution

4K, like SimpleStyle: on Z-DON the skin uses `screen.texture_density = 2` (layouts in 1920x1080
coordinates, every texture authored at twice the size). This skin ships its own graphics, scripts and
font: the engine loads a parent skin's textures at density 1, reads the font from the skin itself, and a
parent's scripts would keep loading the parent's (light) palette.

## Characters

The same two original pixel characters as SimpleStyle, unchanged:

- **Momo**, a pink rabbit with a mint belly: the main character and player 1.
- **Hoshi**, a husky pup in a grey hoodie: the buddy and player 2.

## Fonts

- **Fusion Pixel Font 12px** (https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font), licensed under the
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence texts are in `Fonts/OFL.txt` and `Fonts/LICENSES/`.
  All text, baked or drawn by the game, uses this one family.

## Contents

- `Graphics/`: every texture, drawn by the same generator script as SimpleStyle with its dark palette
  (`python build.py --theme dark`, then `tools/make_dark.py` keeps SimpleStyle's later hand fixes).
- `Scripts/`: SimpleStyle's Lua scripts with the dark palette and text colours. Some are adapted from
  the parent skins (YataiDONNijiiro / PyTaikoGreen) with their art and timelines replaced; credit for the
  adapted parts goes to their authors. The rest are original.
